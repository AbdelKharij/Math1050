\begin{problem}
Translate into an equation and solve. 
 
 9. The difference between a number and fifteen is seven. Find the number. 

 $\answer{22}$
 
 11. The product of seven and a number is negative twenty-one. Find the number.
 
 $\answer{-3}$
 
 13. Four less than three times a number is five. Find the number. 

 $\answer{3}$
 
 15. Four times the sum of twice a number and three is twelve. Find the number. 

 $\answer{0}$
 
 17. Twelve is six times the difference between a number and three. Find the number. 

 $\answer{5}$
 
 19. Twenty-two is two less than six times a number. Find the number. 

 $\answer{4}$
 
 21. Seven more than four times a number is three more than two times the number. Find the number. 

 $\answer{-2}$
 
 23. Eight less than five times a number is four more than eight times the number. Find the number. 

 $\answer{-4}$
 
 25. Twice the difference between a number and twenty-five is three times the number. Find the number. 

 $\answer{-50}$
 
 27. The sum of two numbers is twenty. Three times the smaller is equal to two times the larger. Find the two numbers. 

 The smaller number is $\answer{8}$ and the larger number is $\answer{12}.$
 
 29. The sum of two numbers is eighteen. The total of three times the smaller and twice the larger is forty-four. Find the two numbers. 


 The smaller number is $\answer{8}$ and the larger number is $\answer{10}$.

 31. The sum of two numbers is fourteen. One number is ten more than the other number. 
 Write two different equations that can be used to find the numbers by first writing an equation in which \( n \) represents the larger number and then writing an equation in which \( n \) represents the smaller number.

 35. Explain how to represent three consecutive integers using only one variable. 
 
 37. The sum of three consecutive integers is 54 . Find the integers.  

 The smallest number is $\answer{17}$, the number in the middle is $\answer{18}$, and the largest number is $\answer{19}$.
 
 39. The sum of three consecutive even integers is 84 . Find the integers. 
 

 The smallest number is $\answer{26}$, the number in the middle is $\answer{28}$, and the largest number is $\answer{30}$.

 41. The sum of three consecutive odd integers is 87 . Find the integers. 


 The smallest number is $\answer{27}$, the number in the middle is $\answer{29}$, and the largest number is $\answer{31}$.
 
 43. Find two consecutive even integers such that five times the first integer is equal to four times the second integer.  
 

 The smaller number is $\answer{8}$ and the larger number is $\answer{10}$.
 
 45. Nine times the first of two consecutive odd integers equals seven times the second. Find the integers. 


 The smaller number is $\answer{7} $ and the larger number is$ \answer{9}.$
 
 47. Find three consecutive integers whose sum is negative twenty-four. 

The smallest number is $\answer{-9},$ the middle number is$ \answer{-8}$ and the largest number is$ \answer{-7}.$
 
 49. Three times the smallest of three consecutive even integers is two more than twice the largest. Find the integers.

 

The smallest number is $\answer{10},$ the middle number is $\answer{12}$ and the largest number is $\answer{14}.$

\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{43.} \textbf{Safety} Recently, the National Safety Council collected data on the
  leading causes of accidental death. The findings revealed that for people age 20,
  30 died from a fall, 47 from fire, 200 from drowning, and 1950 from motor vehicle
  accidents. What percent of the accidental deaths were not attributed to motor vehicle
  accidents? Round to the nearest percent.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{45.} \textbf{Energy} The Energy Information Administration reports that if every U.S.
  household switched 4 h of lighting per day from incandescent bulbs to compact
  fluorescent bulbs, we would save 31.7 billion kilowatt-hours of electricity a year,
  or 33\% of the total electricity used for home lighting. What is the total electricity
  used for home lighting in this country? Round to the nearest tenth of a billion.
\end{problem}

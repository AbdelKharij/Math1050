\begin{problem}
\textbf{5.} Two friends are standing 50 ft apart and begin walking toward each other on a
  straight sidewalk. When they meet, what is the total distance covered by the two
  friends?

The total distance covered is $\answer{50}$ ft .
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{7.} Two planes start from the same point and fly in opposite directions. The first
  plane is flying 25 mph slower than the second plane. In 2 h, the planes are 470 mi
  apart. Find the rate of each plane.

The speed of the first plane is $\answer{105}$ mph . The speed of the second plane is $\answer{130}$ mph .
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{9.} One speed skater starts across a frozen lake at an average speed of 8 m/s. Ten
  seconds later, a second speed skater starts from the same point and skates in the same
  direction at an average speed of 10 m/s. How many seconds after the second skater
  starts will the second skater overtake the first skater?

The second skater overtakes the first after $\answer{40}$ seconds.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{11.} Michael Chan leaves a dock in his motorboat and travels at an average speed of
  9 mph toward the Isle of Shoals, a small island off the coast of Massachusetts. Two
  hours later, a tour boat leaves the same dock and travels at an average speed of 18 mph
  toward the same island. How many hours after the tour boat leaves will Michael's boat be
  alongside the tour boat?

Michael's boat will be alongside the tour boat in $ \answer{2}$ hours.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{13.} An executive drove from home at an average speed of 30 mph to an airport where a
  helicopter was waiting. The executive boarded the helicopter and flew to the corporate
  offices at an average speed of 60 mph. The entire distance was 150 mi. The entire trip
  took 3 h. Find the distance from the airport to the corporate offices.

The airport is $\answer{120}$ miles from the corporate offices.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{15.} After a sailboat had been on the water for 3 h, a change in the wind direction
  reduced the average speed of the boat by 5 mph. The entire distance sailed was 57 mi.
  The total time spent sailing was 6 h. How far did the sailboat travel in the first 3 h?

The sailboat traveled $\answer{36}$ miles in the first $\answer{3}$ h .
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{17.} A passenger train leaves a train depot 2 h after a freight train leaves the same
  depot. The freight train is traveling 20 mph slower than the passenger train. Find the
  rate of each train if the passenger train overtakes the freight train in 3 h.

The passenger train is traveling at $ \answer{50}$ miles per hour.  The freight train is traveling at $\answer{30}$ miles per hour .
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{19.} A ship traveling east at 25 mph is 10 mi from a harbor when another ship leaves
  the harbor traveling east at 35 mph. How long does it take the second ship to catch up
  to the first ship?

It takes the second ship $\answer{1}$ hour. to catch up to the first ship.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{21.} At noon, a train leaves Washington, D.C., headed for Charleston, South Carolina, a
  distance of 500 mi. The train travels at a speed of 60 mph. At 1 \textsc{p.m.}, a second
  train leaves Charleston headed for Washington, D.C., traveling at 50 mph. How long after
  the train leaves Charleston will the two trains pass each other?

The two trains will pass each other in $\answer{4}$ hours .
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{23.} A race car driver starts along a 50-mile race course traveling at an average speed
  of 90 mph. Fifteen minutes later, a second driver starts along the same course at an
  average speed of 120 mph. Will the second car overtake the first car before the drivers
  reach the end of the course?

\begin{multipleChoice}
\choice{Yes, the second car will overtake the first car.}
\choice[correct]{No, the second car will not overtake the first car. }
\end{multipleChoice}
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{25.} A bus traveling at a rate of 60 mph overtakes a car traveling at a rate of 45 mph.
  If the car had a 1-hour head start, how far from the starting point does the bus
  overtake the car?

The bus overtakes the car $\answer{180}$ miles from the starting point.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{27.} A plane left Kennedy Airport Tuesday morning for a 605-mile, 5-hour trip. For the
  first part of the trip, the average speed was 115 mph. For the remainder of the trip,
  the average speed was 125 mph. How long did the plane fly at each speed?

The plane flew $\answer{2}$ hours at 115 mph and $\answer{3}$ hours at 125 mph .
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{29.} A cyclist and a jogger set out at 11 \textsc{a.m.} from the same point, headed in
  the same direction. The average speed of the cyclist is twice the average speed of the
  jogger. In 1 h, the cyclist is 7 mi ahead of the jogger. Find the rate of the cyclist.

The rate of the cyclist is $\answer{14}$ miles per hou .
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{31.} At 10 \textsc{a.m.}, two campers left their campsite by canoe and paddled
  downstream at an average speed of 12 mph. They then turned around and paddled back
  upstream at an average rate of 4 mph. The total trip took 1 h. At what time did the
  campers turn around downstream?

The campers turned around downstream at $ \answer{15}$ minutes past 10 a.m.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{33.} A truck leaves a depot at 11 \textsc{a.m.} and travels at a speed of 45 mph. At
  noon, a van leaves the same depot and travels the same route at a speed of 65 mph. At
  what time does the van overtake the truck?

The van overtakes the truck at \( \answer{2:15} \) P.M. (enter your answer with the : in between the hour and the minute)
\end{problem}

 SECTION 4.7 
 
 10. \textbf{Integer Problem} Three-fifths of a number is greater than two-thirds. Find
  the smallest integer that satisfies the inequality.

$\answer{2} $
 
 11. \textbf{Integer Problem} Four times the sum of a number and five is less than six
  times the number. Find the smallest integer that satisfies the inequality.

$ \answer{11} $ 

 12. \textbf{Health} A health official recommends a maximum cholesterol level of 220
  units. A patient has a cholesterol level of 275. By how many units must this patient's
  cholesterol level be reduced to satisfy the recommended maximum level?

$\answer{55}$ units.
 
 13. The couple's monthly household income is \( \answer{5395} \) dollars or more. 
 
 14. \textbf{Sports} To be eligible for a basketball tournament, a basketball team must
  win at least 60\% of its remaining games. If the team has 17 games remaining, how many
  games must the team win to qualify for the tournament?

$\answer{11}$ games.

 15. The organization must collect more than \( \answer{440} \) pounds of cans. 
 
 17. The student must score \(\answer{ 78} \) or better. 
 
 19. The dollar amount in sales must be more than \(  \answer{5714} \) dollars.
 
 21. The height is \( \answer{45} \) feet. 
 
 23. The dollar amount the agent expects to sell is \( \answer{20,000} \) dollars or less (use the comma in the number).
 
 25. \textbf{Nutrition} For a product to be labeled orange juice, a state agency
  requires that at least 80\% of the drink be real orange juice. How many ounces of
  artificial flavors can be added to 32 oz of real orange juice if the drink is to be
  labeled orange juice?

\( \answer{8} \) ounces or less must be added. 
 
 27. \textbf{Mixtures} A silversmith combines 120 oz of an alloy that costs \$4 per
  ounce with an alloy that costs \$1.50 per ounce. How many ounces of the alloy that costs
  \$1.50 per ounce should be used to make a mixture that costs less than \$3 per ounce?

More than \( \answer{80} \) ounces of the alloy must be used. 
 
 29. The ski area is more than \( \answer{38} \) miles away. 
 
 31. The maximum number of miles you can drive is \( \answer{166}\) . 
 
 33. The integers are either (from smallest to largest ) $ \answer{1},\answer{3},\answer{5}$ or $\answer{3},\answer{5}, \answer{7}$ . 
 
 35. The crew can prepare $\answer{8}$ to $\answer{12}$ aircraft in this period of time. 
 
 37. More than $\answer{50}$ ounces of pretzels should be used. 
 
 39. Between \( \answer{4/3} \) (enter as fraction) and \( 30\) pounds of the black tea should be used.

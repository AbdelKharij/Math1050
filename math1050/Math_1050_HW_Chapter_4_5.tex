 15. A goldsmith combined an alloy that costs \$4.30 per ounce with an alloy that costs
  \$1.80 per ounce. How many ounces of each were used to make a mixture of 200 oz costing
  \$2.50 per ounce?

\( \answer{56} \)  ounces of the  \$ 4.30 alloy and \( \answer{144} \) ounces of the \$1.80 alloy were used. 
 
 17. How many pounds of chamomile tea that costs \$18.20 per pound must be mixed with
  12 lb of orange tea that costs \$12.25 per pound to make a mixture that costs \$14.63
  per pound?

\( \answer{8} \) pounds of chamomile tea are needed. 
 
 19. The cost per ounce is \( \answer{5} \) dollars.
 
 21. To make the mixture, \( \answer{3} \) pounds of caramel are needed. 
 
 23. A lumber company combined oak wood chips that cost \$3.10 per pound with pine wood
  chips that cost \$2.50 per pound. How many pounds of each were used to make an
  80-pound mixture costing \$2.65 per pound?

\( \answer{20} \) lb of oak chips and \( \answer{60} \) lb of pine chips were used. 
 
 25. A caterer made an ice cream punch by combining fruit juice that cost \$4.50 per
  gallon with ice cream that cost \$8.50 per gallon. How many gallons of each were used
  to make 100 gal of punch costing \$5.50 per gallon?

\( \answer{75} \)  gal of fruit juice and \( \answer{25} \) gal of ice cream were used.

 43. How many gallons of a 15\% acid solution must be mixed with 5 gal of a 20\% acid
  solution to make a 16\% acid solution?

\( \answer{20} \) gal of the \( 15 \% \) solution must be mixed with \( \answer{5} \) gal of the \( 20 \% \) solution. 
 
 45. A rug is made by weaving 20 lb of yarn that is 50\% wool with a yarn that is 25\%
  wool. How many pounds of the yarn that is 25\% wool must be used if the finished rug is
  to be 35\% wool?

\( \answer{30} \) pounds of \( 25 \% \) wool yarn is used. 
 
 47. How many gallons of a plant food that is 9\% nitrogen must be combined with
  another plant food that is 25\% nitrogen to make 10 gal of a plant food that is 15\%
  nitrogen?

\(\answer{6.25}\) gallons of the plant food that is \( 9 \% \) nitrogen are combined with the plant food that is \( 25 \% \) nitrogen. 
 
 49. The percent concentration is \( \answer{19}  \) percent. 
 
 51. To make the potpourri mixture sold at a florist shop, 70 oz of a potpourri that is
  80\% lavender are combined with a potpourri that is 60\% lavender. The resulting
  potpourri is 74\% lavender. How much of the potpourri that is 60\% lavender is used?

\( \answer{30} \) ounces of the potpourri that is \( 60 \% \) lavender are used. 
 
 53. To make the solution, \( \answer{100} \) ml of the \( 7 \% \) solution and \( \answer{200} \) ml of the \( 4 \% \) solution are used.

 63. A grocer combined walnuts that cost \$5.60 per pound and cashews that cost \$7.50
  per pound with 20 lb of peanuts that cost \$4.00 per pound. Find the amount of walnuts
  and the amount of cashews used to make the 50-pound mixture costing \$5.72 per pound.

$\answer{10}$ pounds of walnuts and $\answer{20}$ pounds of cashews were used.

  \$2.75 for children. A total of 120 tickets were sold for \$563.75. How many adults and

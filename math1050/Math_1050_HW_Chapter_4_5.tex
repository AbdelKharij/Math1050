\begin{problem}
\textbf{15.} A goldsmith combined an alloy that costs \$4.30 per ounce with an alloy that costs
  \$1.80 per ounce. How many ounces of each were used to make a mixture of 200 oz costing
  \$2.50 per ounce?

\( \answer{56} \)  ounces of the  \$ 4.30 alloy and \( \answer{144} \) ounces of the \$1.80 alloy were used.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{17.} How many pounds of chamomile tea that costs \$18.20 per pound must be mixed with
  12 lb of orange tea that costs \$12.25 per pound to make a mixture that costs \$14.63
  per pound?

\( \answer{8} \) pounds of chamomile tea are needed.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{19.} Find the cost per ounce of a mixture of 200 oz of a cologne that costs \$7.50 per
  ounce and 500 oz of a cologne that costs \$4.00 per ounce.

The cost per ounce is \( \answer{5} \) dollars.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{21.} A snack food is made by mixing 5 lb of popcorn that costs \$.80 per pound with
  caramel that costs \$2.40 per pound. How much caramel is needed to make a mixture that
  costs \$1.40 per pound?

To make the mixture, \( \answer{3} \) pounds of caramel are needed.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{23.} A lumber company combined oak wood chips that cost \$3.10 per pound with pine wood
  chips that cost \$2.50 per pound. How many pounds of each were used to make an
  80-pound mixture costing \$2.65 per pound?

\( \answer{20} \) lb of oak chips and \( \answer{60} \) lb of pine chips were used.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{25.} A caterer made an ice cream punch by combining fruit juice that cost \$4.50 per
  gallon with ice cream that cost \$8.50 per gallon. How many gallons of each were used
  to make 100 gal of punch costing \$5.50 per gallon?

\( \answer{75} \)  gal of fruit juice and \( \answer{25} \) gal of ice cream were used.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{27.} Find the cost per pound of a ``house blend'' of coffee that is made from 12 lb of
  Central American coffee that costs \$8 per pound and 30 lb of South American coffee
  that costs \$4.50 per pound.

The cost per pound of the house blend is \(  \answer{5.50} \) dollars.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{31.} Find the cost per ounce of a gold alloy made from 25 oz of pure gold that costs
  \$1282 per ounce and 40 oz of an alloy that costs \$900 per ounce.

The cost per ounce is \(  \answer{1046.92} \) dollars.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{41.} Forty ounces of a 30\% gold alloy are mixed with 60 oz of a 20\% gold alloy. Find
  the percent concentration of the resulting gold alloy.

The percent concentration is \( \answer{24} \) percent.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{43.} How many gallons of a 15\% acid solution must be mixed with 5 gal of a 20\% acid
  solution to make a 16\% acid solution?

\( \answer{20} \) gal of the \( 15 \% \) solution must be mixed with \( \answer{5} \) gal of the \( 20 \% \) solution.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{45.} A rug is made by weaving 20 lb of yarn that is 50\% wool with a yarn that is 25\%
  wool. How many pounds of the yarn that is 25\% wool must be used if the finished rug is
  to be 35\% wool?

\( \answer{30} \) pounds of \( 25 \% \) wool yarn is used.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{47.} How many gallons of a plant food that is 9\% nitrogen must be combined with
  another plant food that is 25\% nitrogen to make 10 gal of a plant food that is 15\%
  nitrogen?

\(\answer{6.25}\) gallons of the plant food that is \( 9 \% \) nitrogen are combined with the plant food that is \( 25 \% \) nitrogen.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{49.} Five grams of sugar are added to a 45-gram serving of a breakfast cereal that is
  10\% sugar. What is the percent concentration of sugar in the resulting mixture?

The percent concentration is \( \answer{19}  \) percent.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{51.} To make the potpourri mixture sold at a florist shop, 70 oz of a potpourri that is
  80\% lavender are combined with a potpourri that is 60\% lavender. The resulting
  potpourri is 74\% lavender. How much of the potpourri that is 60\% lavender is used?

\( \answer{30} \) ounces of the potpourri that is \( 60 \% \) lavender are used.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{53.} A hair dye is made by blending a 7\% hydrogen peroxide solution and a 4\% hydrogen
  peroxide solution. How many milliliters of each are used to make a 300-milliliter
  solution that is 5\% hydrogen peroxide?

To make the solution, \( \answer{100} \) ml of the \( 7 \% \) solution and \( \answer{200} \) ml of the \( 4 \% \) solution are used.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{55.} At a cosmetics company, 40 L of pure aloe cream are mixed with 50 L of a
  moisturizer that is 64\% aloe. What is the percent concentration of aloe in the
  resulting mixture?

The percent concentration is \( \answer{80} \) percent.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{59.} A recipe for a rice dish calls for 12 oz of a rice mixture that is 20\% wild rice
  and 8 oz of pure wild rice. What is the percent concentration of wild rice in the
  20-ounce mixture?

The percent concentration is \( \answer{52} \) percent.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{63.} A grocer combined walnuts that cost \$5.60 per pound and cashews that cost \$7.50
  per pound with 20 lb of peanuts that cost \$4.00 per pound. Find the amount of walnuts
  and the amount of cashews used to make the 50-pound mixture costing \$5.72 per pound.

\textbf{Answer:} $\answer{10}$ pounds of walnuts and $\answer{20}$ pounds of cashews were used.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{65.} A chemist mixed pure acid with water to make 10 L of a 30\% acid solution. How
  much pure acid and how much water did the chemist use?

The chemist used $\answer{3}$ liters of pure acid and $\answer{7}$ liters of water.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{66.} How many grams of pure water must be added to 50 g of pure acid to make a solution
  that is 40\% acid?

\textbf{Answer:} $\answer{75}$
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{67.} Tickets to a performance by a community theater company cost \$5.50 for adults and
  \$2.75 for children. A total of 120 tickets were sold for \$563.75. How many adults and
  how many children attended the performance?

\textbf{Answer:} $\answer{85}$ adults and $\answer{35}$ children attended the performance.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}
\textbf{7.} Use this system of equations: \quad
  $\begin{aligned}(1)\quad y &= 3x - 5\\ (2)\quad x &= 2\end{aligned}$
  \\[3pt]
  To solve the system by substitution, substitute \blank{} for $x$ in equation~(1):
  $y = 3(2) - 5 = \underline{\hspace{2.2em}}$.
  \\[2pt]
  The solution of the system of equations is $(\underline{\hspace{2.2em}}, \underline{\hspace{2.2em}})$.

Fill in the blank:  $\answer{2}$ and $\answer{1}$

Answer as an ordered pair:
$\answer{(2, 1)}$
\end{problem}

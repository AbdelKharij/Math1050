 11. A dentist invested a portion of \$15,000 in a 7\% annual simple interest account
  and the remainder in a 6.5\% annual simple interest government bond. The two

 13. A professional athlete deposited an amount of money into a high-yield mutual fund
  that earns 13\% annual simple interest. A second deposit, \$2500 more than the first,
  was placed in a certificate of deposit earning 7\% annual simple interest. In one year,
  the total interest earned on both investments was \$475. How much money was invested in
  the mutual fund?

\( \answer{ 1500 } \) dollars was invested in the mutual fund.

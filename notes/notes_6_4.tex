\begin{enumerate}
  \item A 600 -mile trip from one city to another takes 4 hours when a plane is flying with the wind. The return trip against the wind takes 5 hours. Find the rate of the plane in still air and the rate of the wind. (NOTE: $r \cdot t=d$, rate $\times$ time $=$ distance)
\end{enumerate}

With wind: 600 mi in 4 h \\
Against wind: 600 mi in 5 h

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|}
\hline
 & Rate & Time & Distance \\
\hline
WITH the wind & $r+w$ & 4 & 600 \\
\hline
AGAINST the wind & $r-w$ & 5 & 600 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{multline*}
4(r+w)=600 \\
5(r-w)=600 \\
r+w=150 \\
\frac{5(r-w)}{5}=\frac{600}{5} \\
r-w=120
\end{multline*}

\begin{multline*}
r+w=150 \\
r-w=120 \\
2r=270 \\
r=135
\end{multline*}

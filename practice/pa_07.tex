N. (2 pts) Simplify: $4 x-2(3 x+8)$

$$\begin{aligned}
& 4 x-2(3 x+8) \\
& 4 x-2(3 x \cdot 4 x+8 \cdot-2) \\
& 12 x^{2}-16
\end{aligned}$$
